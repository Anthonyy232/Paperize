Privacy policy

Introduction

Welcome to Paperize! This privacy notice outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application. By using Paperize, you agree to the terms described in this notice.

Data collection

Notifications: Paperize requests permission to display its own wallpaper-change, background-operation, and error notifications. It does not read notifications from other apps or use them to recommend wallpapers.

Local files: Paperize accesses the images and folders you select so it can set them as wallpapers. We do not upload or transfer these files to any external servers.

Information usage

Personal data: we do not collect or store any personal data such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

Usage analytics: Paperize does not track your usage behavior or collect analytics data.

Data security

On-device storage: all data used by Paperize remains on your device. We do not transmit or store any data externally.

Third-party services

Advertisements: Paperize does not display third-party ads.

Children's privacy

Paperize is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Android permissions

  • SET_WALLPAPER: to set the home and lock screen wallpaper.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show Paperize's own notifications. Optional.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC and WAKE_LOCK: to finish a wallpaper change in the background without Android stopping it midway.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to restore the wallpaper schedule after the device restarts.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: to let you exempt Paperize from battery optimisation so scheduled changes arrive on time. Optional.

Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
