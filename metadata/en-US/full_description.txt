Paperize changes your home and lock screen wallpaper on a schedule, from albums you build out of your own images and folders. It works offline and has no internet permission.

Albums: an album holds single images, whole folders, or both. Folders are rescanned when you open the app, and daily while a schedule is running, so new files join the rotation by themselves.

Schedule: turn on the lock screen, the home screen or both, and pick an album for each. One interval covers both screens, from 15 minutes to 30 days, or each screen can have its own. Wallpapers go in order or shuffled.

Effects: brightness, blur, vignette and a gray filter, each with a strength slider and separate switches for home and lock. Adaptive brightness adjusts the wallpaper to light or dark mode. Scaling is Fill, Fit, Stretch or None.

Live wallpaper mode: instead of ordinary static wallpapers, Paperize can run as a live wallpaper that crossfades between images, changes on a double tap or when the screen turns off, moves with your home-screen pages (parallax), and allows intervals down to 1 minute.

Change now: skip to the next wallpaper from the app, a Quick Settings tile or the app icon shortcut.

Formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, BMP, GIF and TIFF.

Privacy: albums and settings stay on your device. There are no ads, analytics or accounts.
